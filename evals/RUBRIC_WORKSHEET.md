## The rule

Every row gets a decision. For rows recording a change already made,
that is **confirm** or **reverse**. For rows still open, it is
**accept** / **fix** / **drop**.

**Nothing is dropped silently.** A drop with no reason recorded is not a
drop, it is a deletion. The reason column is filled on the same pass as
the decision. This has already gone wrong once, across
47 criteria at once, which is why Batches 0
and 1 exist at all.

Batches are independent. Stop after any one of them.
